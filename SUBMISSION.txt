Title
StableRoster v2 — Stable pairing with finite bilateral consent

Notes / Description
Built StableRoster v2, a reusable MIT-licensed GenLayer Intelligent Contract for bilateral matching. Validators independently derive complete fit rankings from bounded public profiles under a frozen policy, strictly validate integer permutations, and agree on every normalized field. Deterministic Gale-Shapley creates stable pairs; only each paired wallet can accept its own assignment. All paired members must consent to seal; decline, owner withdrawal or permissionless expiry closes the roster. A maximum seven-day deadline, nonzero unique wallets, immutable term digests and terminal-state guards prevent stuck or rewritten records. Lint, strict typechecking, 25 direct tests, 4 five-validator local lifecycle scenarios and 11 offline tooling tests pass. Fresh live v2 proof is pending; the August test reached awaiting responses only. No funds, identity or profile-authenticity attestation.
