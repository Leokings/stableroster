Title
StableRoster v2 — Stable pairing with finite bilateral consent

Notes / Description
StableRoster v2 is an MIT-licensed reusable GenLayer contract for bilateral matching and finite consent. Validators independently rank bounded public profiles under a frozen policy, validate complete integer permutations, and agree on every normalized field. Deterministic Gale-Shapley creates stable pairs; only each paired wallet can accept. All paired members must consent to seal. Decline, owner withdrawal or permissionless expiry closes the roster. Nonzero unique wallets, a maximum seven-day deadline, immutable terms digests and terminal guards prevent stuck or rewritten records.

A fresh StudioNet deployment proved SEALED, DECLINED, WITHDRAWN and EXPIRED lifecycles plus a profile-injection case, with 10 finalized expected rejections. Includes 25 direct tests, 4 five-validator local scenarios, 15 offline tooling tests, exact deployed-source proof and a wallet-free verifier. Profiles are supplied text, not verified identities or true preferences. No funds move.
