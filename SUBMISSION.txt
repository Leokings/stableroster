Title
StableRoster - Bilateral stable roster construction

Notes / Description
Built StableRoster, a reusable GenLayer intelligent contract for bilateral stable roster construction. Consensus produces complete preference matrices for two cohorts; deterministic Gale-Shapley matching creates pairs that require member responses. One deployment supports multiple independently keyed records and callers. Inputs are public, caller-supplied snapshots; the contract does not automatically fetch or authenticate sources and moves no funds. GenVM lint, strict type checking, 4 direct tests and one five-validator GLSim flow passed. StudioNet verified successful finalized intelligent execution, final-state readback and matching deployed source.
